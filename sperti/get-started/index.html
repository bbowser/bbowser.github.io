Step 2 of 2  ·  Medical Screening

A few medical questions

UVB phototherapy is safe for most people. However, certain conditions and medications need extra consideration. Please answer honestly — your safety is the priority.

Have you ever been diagnosed with skin cancer or melanoma — or are you at high risk due to family history?
Do you have lupus (SLE), xeroderma pigmentosum, or any condition that causes extreme UV light sensitivity?
Are you currently taking any medications that increase sensitivity to UV light?
Common examples: psoralen, certain antibiotics (tetracycline, doxycycline), some diuretics, St. John's Wort, retinoids, or certain antidepressants. Ask your pharmacist if unsure.
Do you have a large number of unusual moles, active open skin lesions, or a history of frequent severe sunburns?
Are you currently pregnant, or have you recently undergone radiation therapy?
